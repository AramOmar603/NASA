# Ultimate Commander science notes

- **Day 6 thermal control.** A cooling loop moves heat away from equipment and living spaces. The simulated temperature warning tracks an educational objective. LAMOON does not estimate a cabin temperature or deduct health for this exercise. [NASA crewed spacecraft thermal control](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20080026002)
- **Day 12 communications.** Lunar relay networks can carry messages between lunar systems and Earth. The simulated console shows relay state and leaves survival stocks alone. [NASA LunaNet](https://www.nasa.gov/communicating-with-missions/lunanet/)
- **Day 19 planning.** The player compares stored energy and supplies against configured daily demand for the living crew. The estimates assume no production and are useful for comparing reserves. The exercise applies no new allocation or stock changes. [NASA Moon Base Systems](https://www.nasa.gov/moonbase-systems/)
- **Day 26 emergency.** The switches, intake and routing valve are training scenarios represented by the existing solar, oxygen-generator and recycler conditions. The engine uses its existing chemical and resource abstractions. A working generator needs electricity and a water feed in reality. Recovered water needs treatment and quality checks. [NASA ECLSS](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/)
- **Environment and balance.** Lunar equipment, people and facilities face extreme temperatures, abrasive dust and radiation. The scheduled events, 185 L starting water, 4% daily incident chance, and kit budget are game parameters. Three of five starting kits are reserved for Day 26. The emergency begins after the day's resource draw, giving players time to respond. A mission day is one simulated planning tick. [NASA Lunar South Pole environment](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/moonbase-environment/)
